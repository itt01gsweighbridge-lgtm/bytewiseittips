## What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored by your browser. “Similar technologies” include local storage, which lets a website remember settings on your device. Some are essential for a site to work; others, such as analytics and advertising cookies, are optional.

## Essential storage (always active)

| Name | Type | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| `bw-theme` | Local storage | Remembers whether you chose light or dark mode | Until you clear it |
| `bw-consent` | Local storage | Remembers your cookie choices so we don’t ask on every page | Until you clear it |

These do not track you and are never sent to us.

## Analytics cookies (optional, only if enabled and consented)

| Name | Provider | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| `_ga` | Google Analytics 4 | Distinguishes visitors to produce anonymous usage statistics | Up to 2 years |
| `_ga_<container-id>` | Google Analytics 4 | Maintains session state for statistics | Up to 2 years |

## Advertising cookies (optional, only if advertising is enabled)

When Google AdSense advertising is active, Google and its partners may set cookies such as `__gads`, `__gpi`, `IDE` and `NID` to deliver and measure ads, limit how often you see the same ad, and prevent fraud. If you consent to personalised advertising, these cookies may also be used to show ads based on your interests. A full description is available in Google’s [advertising technologies policy](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).

For visitors in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, a Google-certified consent message asks for your choices before personalised advertising cookies are used.
