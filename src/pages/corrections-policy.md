## How to report an error

Email [{{ site.owner.contactEmail }}](mailto:{{ site.owner.contactEmail }}) or use our [contact page](/contact/) and choose the topic **“Correction”**. It helps if you include:

- the address (URL) of the page;
- the sentence or step you believe is wrong;
- what you think is correct, ideally with a link to official documentation;
- the device, operating system and version you are using, if relevant.

## What happens next

1. **Acknowledgement.** We aim to acknowledge correction requests within a few working days.
2. **Review.** An editor checks the report against official sources and, where possible, the current version of the software or device.
3. **Action.** If the guide is wrong, incomplete or out of date, we fix it as quickly as possible. Errors that could cause data loss or a security risk are treated as urgent.
4. **Reply.** We let you know the outcome if you provided an email address.

## How corrections are shown

- **Significant corrections** – a wrong command, an unsafe step or a factual error that changes the advice – are noted at the end of the article with the date and a short description of what changed, and the article’s “Updated” date is revised.
- **Minor changes** – spelling, formatting, broken links, or clarifications that do not change the meaning – are made without a note.
- **Product changes** – when a vendor changes its software (for example, renames a menu), we update the guide and its “Updated” date. This is maintenance rather than a correction.

## Removal of content

If a guide can no longer be made accurate – for example because a product is discontinued – we will update it with a clear notice, redirect it to a better resource, or remove it.

## Our commitment

We never quietly change a guide to hide a significant error. Thank you for helping us keep ByteWise IT Tips accurate.
