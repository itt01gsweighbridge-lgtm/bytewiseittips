## Use at your own risk

Troubleshooting steps can change system settings, remove software or – in rare cases – lead to data loss. Before following any guide:

- **back up important files** (see our guide to the [3-2-1 backup rule](/articles/3-2-1-backup-rule/));
- check whether your device is under warranty, because opening some devices can affect warranty cover;
- if the device belongs to your employer or school, contact your IT department first – they may manage it centrally and prohibit changes;
- disconnect power and follow the manufacturer’s safety instructions before any hardware work. Do not open power supplies, and stop using any battery that is swollen, damaged or hot.
